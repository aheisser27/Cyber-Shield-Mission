⚠️ Level 2: Log Analysis

Situation Report:

You have intercepted a live server log. Hunters know that security teams are busy, so they hide their attacks inside thousands of lines of normal "digital chatter," hoping you'll get tired of reading.

Your Objective

The Hunter tagged their file with the word BOUNTY. You must find it in the log below.

Action Steps

Scrolling line by line will take too long. A good analyst doesn't read every log; they filter out the noise. Think about how you can scan a large document for a specific keyword without using your eyes.

Your web browser has a built-in search tool to filter text. Try pressing Ctrl + F (or Cmd + F on a Mac) to open it, and type your target word.
